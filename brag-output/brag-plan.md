# FinTrack Pro — Launch Video Plan (brag-slim)

## 1. Product Summary & Creative Angle
- **What it is**: FinTrack Pro is an ultra-private, AI-powered personal finance management system that runs 100% locally in the browser with native desktop/mobile installation.
- **Who it is for**: Developers, privacy advocates, power users, and everyday budgeters who refuse to upload their bank statements, transaction logs, and personal net worth to third-party cloud servers.
- **The Angle**: **"Total Financial Sovereignty"**. A direct, punchy contrast between invasive cloud financial apps and FinTrack Pro's local-first, zero-knowledge architecture.
- **Tone**: `polished` / `cinematic` — Dark mode elegance, glowing tech accents, crisp typography, physical micro-interactions, high confidence.
- **Share Caption**: "Your financial data should belong to you. FinTrack Pro: 100% offline, on-device AI, AES-256 encrypted, and installable on any OS in one click."

## 2. Visual Identity & Design Tokens
- **Background**: Deep obsidian midnight (`#0a0f1e`, `#060913`)
- **Cards & Surfaces**: Glassmorphic slate (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`, borders `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)`)
- **Accents**: Electric Indigo (`#6366f1`), Cyan glow (`#06b6d4`), Emerald profit (`#10b981`), Rose expense (`#f43f5e`)
- **Typography**: Inter / System UI sans-serif, bold tabular numerals for financial figures
- **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 (16:9 Landscape, 30fps)
- **Total Duration**: 20.0 seconds (600 frames)

## 3. Audio Direction & Tempo Grid
- **BPM**: 120 BPM (2.0 beats per second; 1 beat = 0.50s = 15 frames at 30fps)
- **Bar**: 4 beats = 2.0s (60 frames)
- **Instrumentation**: Deep 808 sub-bass, syncopated high-tech percussive clicks/shakers, ethereal minor synth chords (Fm7 - Abmaj7 - Dbmaj7 - C7), rising sweep on transitions, and a crisp metallic UI confirmation chime on the final seal.

## 4. Scene-by-Scene Storyboard

### Scene 1: The Hook (0.0s – 3.5s / Frames 0 – 105)
- **Visual**: Minimalist glowing ambient grid. Two stark lines enter with staggered upward kinetic motion:
  - Line 1: "Where does your financial data go?"
  - Line 2 (Highlight): "With FinTrack Pro... nowhere."
- **Motion**: High-speed entrance with ease-out, glowing neon boundary box pulses on beat 4 (2.0s).
- **Sound**: Atmospheric low-frequency rumble, subtle ticking hi-hats accelerating into a sub drop.

### Scene 2: The Reveal & Real Dashboard (3.5s – 8.0s / Frames 106 – 240)
- **Visual**: Zoom-through cut into the live FinTrack Pro dashboard:
  - Metric Cards: **Net Worth: $148,290.00 (+14.2%)**, **Monthly Cash Flow: +$4,120.00**, **Savings Rate: 38%**
  - Smooth interactive SVG cashflow curve drawing across time.
  - Recent live transactions list (Apple Store, Cloudflare, Whole Foods) with real tags and categories.
- **Motion**: Cards cascade in with a staggered 100ms offset; live area chart renders left-to-right.
- **Sound**: Full drum groove drops at frame 120 (4.0s). Rhythmic bassline carries the visual momentum.

### Scene 3: Highlight 1 — On-Device AI & Statement OCR (8.0s – 12.0s / Frames 241 – 360)
- **Visual**: Split focus on intelligent parsing:
  - Visual bank statement/receipt slides into scanner zone.
  - Neon scanning laser beam sweeps down.
  - Instantly parsed: "UPI/SWIGGY-1284 -> Food & Dining (99.4% match)".
  - 3-tier categorization indicator: Learned -> Rule-Based -> On-Device WebLLM.
- **Motion**: Laser sweep at frame 270; instant badge transition from "Raw Unstructured" to "Verified & Balanced".
- **Sound**: Digital scanning sweep SFX layered on beat, synth arpeggio lifts to higher octave.

### Scene 4: Highlight 2 — Native System & AES-256 Vault (12.0s – 16.0s / Frames 361 – 480)
- **Visual**: Glassmorphic shield & installation badge:
  - "Zero Cloud Storage. Zero Tracking."
  - AES-256-GCM Military Grade Local Vault with PBKDF2.
  - Cross-platform install badges: Windows • macOS • Linux • iOS • Android.
  - Interactive click simulation on "Install App" -> switches to glowing "✓ Installed System".
- **Motion**: Shield lock animation seals at frame 420; installation checkmark pops with Emil Kowalski spring easing.
- **Sound**: Heavy resonant kick on frame 420, crisp metallic confirmation chime on install.

### Scene 5: Outro & Punchline (16.0s – 20.0s / Frames 481 – 600)
- **Visual**: Hero logo card:
  - Big glowing typography: **FinTrack Pro**
  - Subtitle: "Personal Finance. Total Sovereignty."
  - Stats Pill: "197 Tests Passing • 100% Offline • Zero Dependencies"
  - Call to Action: "Get Started: github.com/sivak/expense-tracker"
- **Motion**: Elements settle into the final hero lockup. Ambient glowing embers gently drift.
- **Sound**: Synth chords sustain and fade with a warm reverb tail; final kick on beat 38.
